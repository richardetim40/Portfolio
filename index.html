TheraTrack

Voice-to-note clerking records for healthcare students. Record a patient's account on the ward and get a structured SOAP note.

  • Cloudflare Workers
  • D1
  • Workers AI
  • Google sign-in
Case study
Problem
Students on clinical posting need to record patient clerking notes quickly, from a phone, while on the ward.
What I did
Built a phone-first records app on Cloudflare Workers and D1. A student records the patient's account for up to 10 minutes, the app transcribes it and turns it into a SOAP-format note, and the note is saved to the patient's record. Every note is tied to a visit, with session type, visit date and the subjective, objective, assessment and plan sections required. Sign-in works with email or Google, and passwords are hashed in the browser before they reach the server.
Outcome
Live and working end to end: sign-in, voice recording, note generation and saving. Built for healthcare students, with small clinics as the next audience.
